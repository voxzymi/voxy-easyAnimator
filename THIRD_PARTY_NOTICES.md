### upscayl-ncnn (AGPL-3.0): what that means here

The upscaler is a separate program (`tools/upscayl/upscayl-bin.exe`) that Voxy starts as a subprocess and talks to only through
its command line. Voxy does not link it. If it is shipped:

- include the AGPL-3.0 text next to it (done by `installer/fetch_third_party.ps1`),
- offer the corresponding source of the exact binary shipped (pin the commit in `installer/third_party.json`),
- do not change it without publishing the change.

The license was read from the upstream repository's README (https://github.com/upscayl/upscayl-ncnn, "AGPL-3.0 license").
Have the owner confirm whether this arrangement is acceptable for a commercial product.

## Not bundled, on purpose

- **Adobe After Effects SDK**: not redistributable. Each developer downloads it from Adobe.
- **Adobe CEP `CSInterface.js`**: the panel does not use it; it talks to `window.__adobe_cep__` directly.
- **Any anime artwork or third-party dataset**: none is included. The training art is generated by this project.
- **VGG16 ImageNet weights** (for the optional perceptual loss while training the inpainting model): downloaded by the developer; the
  weights' license is not clear-cut, so they are not part of any release.
- **Pretrained matting / inpainting weights** (e.g. anime matting networks, LaMa): see `docs/models.md` for candidates and what to check.
